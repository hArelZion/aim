A REAWAKENED HERO

After a 100-year slumber, Link awakens alone in a secluded chamber, his memories lost. Guided only by a mysterious voice and the Sheikah Slate, he emerges into a vast, ruined kingdom of Hyrule overrun by Calamity Ganon.

Travel across vast fields, through dense forests, and to mountain peaks to discover what became of the ruined kingdom and restore peace before Ganon breaks free completely.

THE SHEIKAH SLATE

An ancient relic equipped with mystical runes. Use Remote Bombs to clear obstacles, Magnesis to manipulate metallic objects, Cryonis to freeze water into ice pillars, and Stasis to freeze object momentum.

Boundless Exploration

Climb any mountain peak, glide over wide valleys, and forge your own path through the expansive lands of Hyrule using dynamic stamina mechanics.

Over 120 Shrines of Trials await. Solve complex physical puzzles, manipulate environmental elements, and earn Spirit Orbs to upgrade health and stamina.
