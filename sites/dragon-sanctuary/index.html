Welcome to the Mountain Dragon Sanctuary

Our Mission

At Aurora Conservation, we are dedicated to safeguarding the fragile ecosystems that sustain our mythical dragon populations. By partnering with local communities, we implement habitat restoration projects, monitor nesting sites, and advocate for sustainable land use. Our goal is to ensure that future generations can witness these majestic creatures thriving in their natural environment, while fostering a deep respect for the delicate balance of our planet’s biodiversity.
