Chef Kenji Sato

"Omakase is a dialogue without words. It is an agreement of ultimate trust between the chef and the guest."

Trained in Ginza, Tokyo, for over a decade, Chef Kenji relocated to Tel Aviv driven by a passion for the vibrant fresh catches of the Levantine coast. At Tokyo Garden, he marries austere Japanese technique with the extraordinary quality of local Mediterranean seafood.


Every piece of nigiri is served at precise body temperature, lightly brushed with aged house-brewed soy sauce, and prepared directly in front of your eyes.
