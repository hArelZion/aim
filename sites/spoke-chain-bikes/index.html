Built On Grease & Grit

Founded in 2014 by former courier Marcus Vance, Spoke & Chain started in a tiny garage with two stands and a single goal: honest, fast, and uncompromising cycle repair for the neighborhood.

We don't do corporate upsells. We diagnose correctly, source quality parts, and treat every bike—whether it's a restored vintage steel frame or a modern carbon road machine—with equal reverence.
